# Account Access & Recovery

## Password reset

On the sign-in page, select "Forgot password?", enter your workspace email, and we send a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. Requesting a new link immediately invalidates the previous one. Reset links can only be used once.

If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and confirm you used the address your workspace owner invited. You can request a new link as often as you need; only the most recent link works.

## Locked accounts

After several failed sign-in attempts the account pauses for 15 minutes as a security measure. The pause lifts automatically — no support contact is required. Workspace owners can also unlock a member early from Settings → Team.

The pause only affects sign-in. Sessions that are already signed in continue to work, and no data is deleted or changed by the pause.

## Two-factor authentication

Admins enable authenticator-app two-factor authentication from Settings → Security. Members scan a QR code with their authenticator app and store the one-time recovery codes somewhere safe. Recovery codes are shown once and can be regenerated from the same screen.

If you lose your authenticator app, a recovery code signs you in; after using one, generate new codes from Settings → Security. Workspace owners cannot read your codes or bypass two-factor authentication on your behalf.

## Changing your email address

Members change their own email address from Settings → Profile. After entering the new address, a confirmation link is sent to it and is valid for 60 minutes; the address is only swapped once the link is used. Until the change is confirmed, the previous address remains active for sign-in and notifications.

When the change completes, the old address receives a notification so an unauthorized change is visible immediately. If you confirm a change you did not make, contact your workspace owner, who can remove and re-invite the member from Settings → Team.

## Sessions & devices

A session stays signed in until it has been inactive for 30 days. Signing in on a new device or browser never signs you out anywhere else, and every session sees the same workspace data.

The active session list — device, browser, and last activity — is in Settings → Security, and "Sign out everywhere" ends every session except the one you are using. Members manage only their own sessions; workspace owners cannot see or end a member's sessions.

## Losing your authenticator device

Only authenticator apps that generate time-based one-time codes are supported; SMS codes are not offered. If your device is lost and you have no recovery codes left, an administrator cannot disable two-factor authentication for you — the protection is intentionally outside admin reach.

Recovery in that situation is a support request: the workspace owner raises a ticket, our team verifies your identity out of band, and two-factor authentication is cleared for a single sign-in window. You immediately enroll a new authenticator app from Settings → Security as part of the same procedure.
