# Account Access & Recovery

## Password reset

On the sign-in page, select "Forgot password?", enter your workspace email, and we send a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. Requesting a new link immediately invalidates the previous one. Reset links can only be used once.

If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and confirm you used the address your workspace owner invited. You can request a new link as often as you need; only the most recent link works.

## Locked accounts

After several failed sign-in attempts the account pauses for 15 minutes as a security measure. The pause lifts automatically — no support contact is required. Workspace owners can also unlock a member early from Settings → Team.

The pause only affects sign-in. Sessions that are already signed in continue to work, and no data is deleted or changed by the pause.

## Two-factor authentication

Admins enable authenticator-app two-factor authentication from Settings → Security. Members scan a QR code with their authenticator app and store the one-time recovery codes somewhere safe. Recovery codes are shown once and can be regenerated from the same screen.

If you lose your authenticator app, a recovery code signs you in; after using one, generate new codes from Settings → Security. Workspace owners cannot read your codes or bypass two-factor authentication on your behalf.
