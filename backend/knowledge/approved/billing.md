# Billing & Subscription Plans

## Billing cycles

Plans bill monthly or annually on the anniversary of your signup date. The first invoice is issued at signup; renewal invoices are sent by email within an hour of each charge.

If a renewal date falls on a weekend or holiday, the charge is processed on the next business day. Changing your plan mid-cycle never changes your billing anniversary.

## Payment methods

Payment methods are managed in Settings → Billing → Payment methods by owners and billing admins. To update or replace the card on file, add the new card there and mark it as the default; renewals then charge the newly updated card. A new card is verified with a small temporary authorization that is released within 3–5 business days before it is used for renewal.

Only one card is charged per cycle. Adding a second card does not split the payment — the card marked as default is used for renewals.

## Invoices & receipts

Every invoice is downloadable from Settings → Billing → Invoices and shows the plan, seat count, and any proration applied. Invoices remain available for seven years and can be emailed to a billing contact automatically.

To add a company name, VAT number, or purchase order number to future invoices, edit the billing profile before the next renewal; already-issued invoices can be reissued with the new details.

## Plans

There are three plans. Starter is free and includes up to 3 members with the core conversation features. Pro is $18 per member per month, or $15 per member per month billed annually, and adds integrations, webhooks, and API access. Enterprise is priced individually and additionally includes single sign-on, the audit log, and daily exports to your own storage.

Every new workspace begins on a 14-day Pro trial with no payment method required, as described in the getting-started guide. Comparing current plan features at any time is possible from Settings → Billing → Plans before you commit to a change.

## Failed payments

If a renewal charge is declined, we email the billing contact and retry the same card after 3 days and again after 7 days. Updating the card in Settings → Billing → Payment methods triggers an immediate retry — no waiting for the next scheduled attempt.

If the charge still has not succeeded after the retries, the workspace enters the read-only retention window described in the data-retention policy. Completing payment during that window restores full access immediately, and nothing is deleted while the window is running.

## Refunds

Annual renewals can be refunded within 14 days of the charge; monthly charges are non-refundable, but cancelling always stops future renewals immediately. Refunds return to the original card within 5–10 business days.

Prorated upgrade charges are payments for service already active, so they are not refundable — downgrading instead takes effect at the next renewal, as described in the subscription guide.

## Taxes

VAT, GST, or sales tax is added to invoices based on the billing country set in the billing profile. Business customers in the EU with a valid VAT ID are reverse-charged: enter the VAT ID in Settings → Billing → Billing profile and tax is removed from the next invoice after validation.

Tax details apply from the next invoice onward; already-issued invoices can be reissued with corrected tax details in the same way as other billing-profile changes.

## Billing contacts

In addition to the invoice emails each owner receives, owners can add billing contacts from Settings → Billing. A billing contact receives renewal invoices and payment-failure notices but cannot sign in to the workspace or change any billing settings — the address is notification-only.

There is no limit on the number of billing contacts, and each one can be removed from the same screen at any time.
