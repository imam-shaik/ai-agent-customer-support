# Billing & Subscription Plans

## Billing cycles

Plans bill monthly or annually on the anniversary of your signup date. The first invoice is issued at signup; renewal invoices are sent by email within an hour of each charge.

If a renewal date falls on a weekend or holiday, the charge is processed on the next business day. Changing your plan mid-cycle never changes your billing anniversary.

## Payment methods

Payment methods are managed in Settings → Billing → Payment methods by owners and billing admins. To update or replace the card on file, add the new card there and mark it as the default; renewals then charge the newly updated card. A new card is verified with a small temporary authorization that is released within 3–5 business days before it is used for renewal.

Only one card is charged per cycle. Adding a second card does not split the payment — the card marked as default is used for renewals.

## Invoices & receipts

Every invoice is downloadable from Settings → Billing → Invoices and shows the plan, seat count, and any proration applied. Invoices remain available for seven years and can be emailed to a billing contact automatically.

To add a company name, VAT number, or purchase order number to future invoices, edit the billing profile before the next renewal; already-issued invoices can be reissued with the new details.
