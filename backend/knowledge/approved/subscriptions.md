# Managing Your Subscription

## Upgrading plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the current cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal so access continues until the period you have paid for ends.

The proration charge appears as a separate line on your next invoice, so the original renewal amount is always visible.

## Adding seats

Seats added mid-cycle are billed only for the days they remain active. Seats can be removed at any time; removed seats stop renewing at the next cycle rather than mid-period.

Members who are removed keep their personal profile, but their conversations remain in the workspace. Re-adding the same person later restores their access without creating a duplicate account.

## Pausing & cancellation

Annual plans can be paused once per year for up to 60 days from Settings → Billing. Cancellations stop future renewals; data remains available in read-only mode for 30 days after the paid period ends.

A pause freezes billing for the length of the window and your data stays exactly as it is — when the window ends, the plan resumes automatically unless you cancel before then.

## When your trial ends

The 14-day Pro trial ends on day 14 at the same time of day the workspace was created. Choosing a plan from Settings → Billing before or on that day keeps every feature active with no interruption, and conversations from the trial period remain in the workspace either way.

If no plan is chosen by the end of the trial, the workspace becomes read-only and follows the standard 30-day retention window described in the data-retention policy. Starting a paid plan during that window restores full access immediately — no data is lost and nothing needs to be imported.

## Cancelling during the trial

Cancelling during the trial ends it immediately; because no payment method is required for the trial, nothing is charged and no invoice is issued. The workspace data then follows the same read-only retention window as an expired trial.

## Switching billing cycle

Moving between monthly and annual billing is made from Settings → Billing → Plans and takes effect at your next renewal — you finish the period you have already paid for on the current cycle. The billing anniversary never moves, so annual plans switched to monthly renew on the same calendar day each month.

The renewal invoice after the switch reflects the new cycle amount, and the change is shown as its own line so the transition is visible on the invoice.

## Downgrading

A downgrade applies at the next renewal, and the workspace keeps working at the current plan until then. When the downgrade takes effect, features above the new plan pause rather than disappear: conversations and history stay intact, and anything the new plan does not include — integrations, webhooks, API access on Starter — resumes unchanged if you upgrade again.

If the new plan's member limit is lower than your current member count, the workspace continues normally, but new invitations pause until you are within the limit again. No members are removed automatically by a downgrade.
