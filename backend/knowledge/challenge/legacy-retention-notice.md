## Retention period

Workspace data is retained for 90 days after the paid period ends before it is permanently deleted. This longer window applies to every workspace that migrated from the legacy platform.

## Historical note

This notice was written for legacy-platform migrations and has not been updated since the retention policy was shortened. It is kept only so the conflicting-evidence challenge can be tested against real retrieved content.
